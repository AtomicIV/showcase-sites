    How a Kai project happens

    01

    Poke it.

    Day one is a rough, working prototype. Not a deck, not a moodboard. Something you can touch and react to.

    Day 1

    Play with it.

    I sketch thirty variations in code, then we keep the two that make people grin. Usually the strangest one wins.

    Week 1–2

    Polish it.

    Timing, easing, accessibility, the 4-year-old Android phone in my drawer. 60 frames per second or it doesn't ship.

    Week 3–5

    Ship it.

    Clean code, a short video walkthrough and a README your team will actually read. Then I stick around for launch week.
