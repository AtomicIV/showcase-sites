Three days, one croissant

Butter, folded 81 times

Monday, 3 pm

The butter block

A slab of cultured butter, beaten flat and chilled, is locked inside a sheet of dough. Three layers so far.

First fold

Roll, fold in three

Rolled long, folded like a letter, rested for half an hour in the cold. Nine layers.

Second fold

Again, colder

If the butter warms up it smears instead of staying in sheets, so the dough goes back in the fridge between every turn. Twenty-seven.

Third fold

Eighty-one layers

The last fold. Each sheet of butter is now thinner than a page of this website would be if you printed it.

Tuesday, 5 am

Proof, then bake

Cut, rolled, proofed overnight and baked for 18 minutes until the layers lift apart and the edges go the colour of toffee.
