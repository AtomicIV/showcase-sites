Aerodynamics

Shaped
by air

Drag coefficient0.300

01 · Front

An air curtain, not a grille

Two slim ducts in the bumper send air around the front wheels instead of into them. Nothing to cool means nothing to open.

02 · Side

Handles that disappear

Flush handles rise as you walk up and sink again once you're moving. Side mirrors are cameras, saving 11 km of range on a full charge.

03 · Rear

A tail cut short on purpose

The roof falls in one line and stops sharp. That clean break lets the air leave without dragging the car back with it.

Interior

Inside,
it's quieter

01

One screen that knows when to hide

A 15.6-inch display that dims to a single line of range and speed at night, and folds its maps away when you park.

02

Seats in recycled wool

Upholstery woven from post-consumer wool and bottle fibre, ventilated and heated, with no animal leather anywhere in the cabin.

03

A roof made of sky

One pane of laminated glass from windscreen to rear seats, coated to block 99% of UV and most of the summer heat.

04

Silence, engineered

Acoustic glass on every window and foam-lined tyres keep the cabin at 59 dB at motorway speed. Conversations stay conversations.
