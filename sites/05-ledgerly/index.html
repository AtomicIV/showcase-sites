06 Questions

Asked, answered.

Can Ledgerly move my money?

Not unless you ask it to. Bank connections are read-only. Goal transfers only happen after you approve a plan, and you can pause them with one tap.

Where does the AI run?

Categorising and the Sunday Brief run on our own servers in the EU. Your transactions are never used to train models for anyone else.

What happens if I cancel?

You drop to the Free plan and keep your history. Delete your account instead and everything is erased within 24 hours.

Is this financial advice?

No. Ledgerly explains your own numbers and helps you plan savings. For investment decisions, talk to a licensed adviser.
