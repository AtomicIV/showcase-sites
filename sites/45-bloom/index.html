    Can I use Bloom with products I already own?

    Yes. Keep what's working. The quiz assumes nothing — if you already love a cleanser, skip ours and start at step two.

    Is it safe for sensitive skin?

    Every formula is fragrance-free, pH-balanced and patch-tested on a sensitive-skin panel. The quiz will steer you away from retinal and acids if you tell us your skin reacts easily.

    How do refills work?

    Keep the glass and the pump. Refill pouches use 80% less plastic and cost 15% less. Send empties back with any order and we'll reuse them.

    What if it doesn't suit me?

    Return any product within 60 days, even if it's half empty. We'd rather know.
