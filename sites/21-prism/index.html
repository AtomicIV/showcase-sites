02 — How it works

How Prism builds an identity

01 / 05

It reads the word out loud.

Open vowels read soft and round, hard consonants read sharp, and length sets the tempo. "Halcyon" lands calm and lyrical. "Zest" lands quick and bright.

02 / 05

Sound becomes a mood.

Three dials — warmth, shape and rhythm — steer every choice after this one. You can nudge any of them before you commit.

03 / 05

One hue grows a palette.

A hero, a support and a spark, plus an ink and a paper tuned to clear 4.5:1 contrast. Five tones you can actually use.

04 / 05

Type with a point of view.

A display face with character for headlines, a text face that disappears into the reading. Paired from a library of tested combinations.

05 / 05

A mark drawn on the grid.

Orbit, petal, facet, grid, wave or monogram — built on a 200-unit grid so it still reads at 16 pixels in a browser tab.
