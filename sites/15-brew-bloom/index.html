Our beans

From cherry to cup, in four slow steps.

  1. 01 · the farm

    Picked ripe in Huila

    We buy direct from the Rojas family at 1,800 metres, paying 40% above the fair-trade floor for cherries picked only when they're wine-red.

  2. 02 · the roast

    Roasted on Tuesdays

    Twelve-kilo batches on our drum roaster by the front window. We stop light enough that you can still taste the orchard.

  3. 03 · the rest

    Rested for six days

    Fresh isn't best. Beans rest in valved bags until the gas settles and the sweetness comes forward.

  4. 04 · the pour

    Poured to order

    Weighed, timed and tasted every morning — then poured slowly, with a little flower on top if you ask nicely.
