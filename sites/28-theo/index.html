        About Theo Marchetti

        I cook the way my nonna argued: slowly, loudly, and with too much butter.

        Theo grew up above his grandmother’s trattoria in the hills outside Modena, peeling potatoes before he could see over the counter. Fifteen years in other people’s kitchens later, he opened Marchetti with a wood oven, a secondhand hearth and a promise to cook only what he’d want to eat himself.

        There’s no à la carte and no freezer. The menu changes when the market does, the bread is baked at four, and the pasta is rolled by hand every afternoon at the long table you’ll eat at that night.
