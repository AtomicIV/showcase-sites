Our rules

Three rules,
no tricks

we wrote these on a napkin and never changed them

3

People a day

Three thoughtful introductions every evening at seven. No bottomless feed, no paying to see more.

1st

Words first

You read someone's answers before you see their face clearly. Photos sharpen once you've said hello.

7

Days to meet

Matched? You have a week to plan a real coffee. If neither of you does, it gently fades. No pen pals.

How it works

From a prompt
to a coffee

one.

Write, don't pose

Answer three prompts in your own words. Our editors read every profile and nudge you if an answer is just “I love travel”.

two.

Meet three people

Every evening at seven you get three people chosen for what you both wrote, not for who looks most like your last swipe.

three.

Reply to something

Like someone by answering one of their prompts. A sentence beats a heart, and it gives the other person something to say back.

four.

Go and get a coffee

Once it's mutual, Kindred suggests a quiet café halfway between you. You have seven days. Most people need two.
