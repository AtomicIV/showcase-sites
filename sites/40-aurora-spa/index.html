The house

About Aurora

Built in 1931 as a summer sanatorium, rebuilt in larch and glass in 2022 by people who mostly wanted to keep the silence intact.

We built Aurora around one idea: that rest is not a reward for working hard, but a place you can arrive at. Slow mornings. Hot water, cold lake. A bed that faces the window, always.
