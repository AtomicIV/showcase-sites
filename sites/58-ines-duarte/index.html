Anatomy of an ampersand

01 · Skeleton

Start with a single line

Every Vinha glyph begins as a hairline drawn with a 2H pencil. If the skeleton is wrong, no amount of weight will save it.

02 · Weight

Then add mass

Ink goes on the strokes that carry the shape. The counters close up, so every bowl is redrawn at nine weights, not interpolated blindly.

03 · Softness

Round the corners

A softness axis takes the edge off the terminals, from bookish and sharp to a little bit like letterpress on damp paper.

04 · Optical size

Fit it to the job

At 9 point the letters get sturdier and wider apart; at 144 they get delicate and tight. One font file, every size you need.
