I · The Firm

Deliberately small. Unusually long-sighted.

We have never advertised, never pitched in public, and never taken more clients than our partners can personally serve. What we offer is the same thing we offered in 1912: an unhurried, unsentimental second opinion from people with nothing to sell you but the truth.

Every engagement is led by a partner from first meeting to final board paper. We publish no league tables and name no clients — the people who need to know us, already do.
