From flash to skin

One swallow,
four passes

i.

Stencil, then line

The stencil goes on, then the outline in one confident pass with a big needle grouping. The line is the tattoo; everything else is decoration.

ii.

Black goes in first

Solid black packed while the skin is fresh. Black is what keeps a traditional tattoo readable after decades of sun and swimming.

iii.

Then the colour

Red, then yellow, packed solid with no gaps. Bold colour inside a bold line holds its shape instead of blurring into the skin.

iv.

Heal, and settle

Two weeks to heal, a month to settle. The colour softens a touch and sits in the skin, and that's the swallow for life.

Shop rules

Five rules,
no exceptions

  1. 18 and over, with IDNo exceptions for birthdays, parents' notes or “I look older”.
  2. €50 deposit holds your dateIt comes off the final price. Move your date once for free with 48 hours' notice.
  3. No copies of other artists' workBring it as inspiration and I'll draw you something of your own.
  4. Eat first, sleep wellA sandwich before your session saves more tattoos than any numbing cream.
  5. Hands, necks and faces by conversationHappy to, once you've got some work elsewhere. Let's talk about it.

Aftercare

Look after
it, it's yours

Day 1

Leave the wrap on for three hours, then wash gently with warm water and unscented soap. Pat dry with clean paper towel.

Days 2–5

A thin layer of unscented balm, three times a day. Thin. It should look shiny, not greasy.

Week 2

It will flake and itch. Don't pick or scratch; slap it gently if you must. No swimming, no sunbeds.

Forever

Sunscreen. Every summer, every time. It's the difference between bold and blurry at sixty.
