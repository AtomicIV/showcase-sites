    05 — How we work

    i.

    Draw the light first.

    We model the sun on the site for a full year before we place a single wall. Rooms go where their hours are.

    ii.

    Build less, better.

    Most clients ask for one room too many. Taking it out pays for the oak floors and the triple glazing.

    iii.

    Leave room for weather.

    Deep eaves, sheltered doors and walls that can get wet. A Swedish house should enjoy November, not survive it.
