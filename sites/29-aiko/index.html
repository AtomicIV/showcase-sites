      05 — About

      About Aiko Mori

      “I like machines that are a little broken. They wobble, they hiss, they forget what you told them — that’s where the songs are.”

      Aiko Mori grew up above her father’s radio-repair shop in Osaka and learned to solder before she learned to read music. She moved to Glasgow to study sound engineering, stayed for the rain, and has released four records of slow, melodic electronics since — most of them mixed at her kitchen table on headphones.

      On the table

      • EchoA 1979 tape echo with a cracked capstan — the wobble on every record
      • DrumsA drum machine rebuilt from two broken ones bought for £20
      • KeysThe Tide-2, a two-oscillator synth she designed with a friend
      • FieldA pocket recorder full of ferry horns, rain and kettles
      • ToyA toy keyboard with a broken C, used on every album since 2019
