An evening with Hearth

On your way home

Hearth sees your phone leave the office and starts warming the living room to 21°. It needs twenty minutes; your train takes twenty-five.

The porch knows you

The porch light comes on as you reach the gate, and the hall lamp follows you in. No app, no fumbling for a switch in the dark.

Lamps soften by themselves

As the evening goes on, every lamp drifts warmer and dimmer, so the house winds down when you do.

Goodnight, in one tap

Touch the stone on your way upstairs: downstairs goes dark, the door locks and the heating settles at 17° until morning.

Quiet by design

Your house,
not our cloud

Everything Hearth decides, it decides on the stone itself. Your routines, voices and comings and goings stay inside your four walls unless you choose otherwise.

Works when the internet doesn't

Lamps, heating schedules and the goodnight scene all keep running through a broadband outage. Only remote access waits for the connection to come back.

No cameras, no microphones

The stone has a touch ring and a light. That's it. Voice control is optional and runs through a speaker you already trust.
