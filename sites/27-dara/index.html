( 02 ) Principles

Four rules Iwork by.

Swipe →

Rule 01Observation 01

Watch before you ask.

People describe the work they think they do. Spend an hour beside them and you see the work they actually do — the sticky note on the monitor, the spreadsheet nobody admits to.

Rule 02Sample size

Five people, not five hundred.

For finding problems, small and often beats big and once. Five sessions this week will teach you more than a survey that lands next quarter.

Rule 03Shelf life

Findings expire.

Markets move, products change, people adapt. Research is a habit with a date on it, not a report you file and quote for three years.

Rule 04Output

Decisions, not decks.

Every study ends with one page: what we learned, what we’ll change, who owns it. If nothing changes, the study wasn’t finished.
