    Sound signature

    Five voices.
    One driver.

    The AA-1 stores five tunings in a DSP the size of a grain of rice, switched from a button on the left cup. Pick one and watch the response reshape the spectrum — or press play to hear a short loop through it. Drag any band to make your own.

    Reference

    Audio starts only when you press play, at low volume. Bands accept arrow keys.

    Materials

    Nothing glued.
    Nothing hidden.

    01 · Yokes

    Billet aluminium

    Machined from a single block of 6063, bead-blasted and anodised to a finish that doesn't fingerprint. The hinge clicks through fourteen positions.

    02 · Cushions

    Lambskin over slow foam

    Magnetic, so they come off in one pull. Ships with a second pair in perforated velour for long sessions.

    03 · Every part

    Held by a screw

    • Driver module
    • Cushions
    • Headband pad
    • Yokes
    • Slider rods
    • Grilles
    • Cable
    • DSP board

    04 · Weight

    312g

    Lighter than it looks, heavier than it feels — the band spreads it across the crown.

    05 · Cable

    Braided, 3.5 mm, both ends

    Two metres of oxygen-free copper in a cotton braid. Standard plugs, so any cable you already own will do.
