For grown-ups

Built for the last ten minutes of the day.

We tested Fable with 240 families over a winter of bedtimes. Here’s what stayed in, and what we threw out.

Screens off, ears on

Tap “lights out” and the screen fades to black while a warm voice reads on. The pictures are for choosing; the story is for listening.

Eight minutes, then quiet

Every story slows down on its last page — shorter sentences, softer words, a yawn or two. No cliffhangers, no “one more”.

Your values, gently

Pick a theme for the week — sharing, trying again, being kind to yourself — and it threads quietly through the stories.

Nothing scary after 7 pm

“Brave” stories stay small and kind: a sound that turns out to be a lost firefly, never a monster. Every draft is checked against our bedtime guidelines before your child hears it.

Keep the good ones

Favourite a story and it joins your family shelf. Twelve favourites? We’ll print them as a real hardback, with your child’s name on the spine.
