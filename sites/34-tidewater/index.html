Research

Three programmes, three depths.

We build long, patient datasets. Every instrument we put in the water stays long enough to see a season change, and every byte is published openly within a year.

  1. 01

    Twilight Census

    Counting the fish of the twilight zone with echosounders fitted to fourteen cargo ships on their normal routes.

    2.1million km of acoustic track
  2. 02

    Abyssal Carbon

    Sediment cores from 5,000 metres, to measure how fast the deep sea locks carbon away and how warming changes that.

    640cores from nine ocean basins
  3. 03

    Trench Watch

    Free-falling landers with baited cameras, left on the trench floor for thirty days at a time and then called home by sound.

    27lander drops below 8,000 m
On station now

RV Halcyon, Kermadec Trench

Thirty days over one of the deepest trenches in the Pacific, dropping the lander Kittiwake to the floor and back, nine times.
