How Velo One is built, part by part

    Four finishes.

    The colour is baked into the frame in six coats, then sealed matte. Pick one and the whole page follows.

    How it rides

    It weighs less than most city bikes without a motor, and it pulls you up the last hill home without making a sound.

    16.8kg

    Size M with lights, lock and pedals. Light enough to carry up a flight of stairs.

    140km

    Range in Eco on a single charge. Around 70 km in Boost with a heavy bag.

    2.5h

    From empty to 80%, with the battery in the bike or out on your desk.

    Specifications

    Motor
    Velo Drive M2 mid-drive, 250 W rated, 85 Nm
    Battery
    540 Wh, removable with a key, in the down tube
    Range
    Up to 140 km in Eco, 70 km in Boost
    Assist up to
    25 km/h in the EU and UK, 32 km/h in the US
    Frame
    Hydroformed 6069 aluminium, sizes S, M and L
    Drivetrain
    Carbon belt with a 5-speed internal hub
    Brakes
    Hydraulic discs, 180 mm rotors front and rear
    Weight
    16.8 kg in size M, ready to ride
    Lights
    80 lux front beam, brake-sensing rear light
    Security
    Frame lock and GPS tracking in the Velo app
