Fig. 02 — How Tessera reads a question

How Tessera reads a question

Which region grew fastest last quarter?

Answer shape

"Which…?"

chart: ranked bars, top one lit

One winner out of many reads best as a sorted bar chart with the answer highlighted.

Dimension

"region"

GROUP BY stores.region

Matched through the semantic layer, which also knows "market" and "territory" mean the same thing here.

Metric

"grew fastest"

ORDER BY revenue_growth DESC

"Grew" means revenue growth by default. One click switches it to orders or customers.

Time window

"last quarter"

order_date in last full quarter

The last complete calendar quarter, not the last 90 days. Your fiscal calendar wins if you have one.
