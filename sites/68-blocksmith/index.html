How it snaps

Five parts,
one click each

1

Every part is a block

Heads, arms and wheels share the same peg grid as every other Blocksmith block, so a robot can grow a tower for a hat.

2

Pegs click at 3 mm

Each peg grips with a short, firm click you can hear. It takes 2.5 kg to pull straight apart, about what a small child can manage, and no more.

3

Ball joints twist, not snap

Shoulders and necks turn on soft ball joints that pop out instead of breaking when a robot falls off the table. They will.

4

Then it's yours

Every part comes in four colours. Mix them, swap them, lose one under the sofa and get a spare for free.
