Minuit, a night in three acts

Act I · 21:00

The opening

Bergamot flares and is gone, like a match struck in a stairwell. Black pepper keeps the spark alive long enough to get you out the door.

Act II · 23:00

The heart

A Damask rose, smoked over cedar for three days, opens slowly against cold iris. It sits close to the skin and asks people to lean in.

Act III · 03:00

What stays

Labdanum, vetiver and tonka: resin and warmth that are still on your collar in the morning, long after the night has a story.
