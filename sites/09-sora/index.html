How a job
gets loud

swipe the cards

01

Scribble

Every job starts with a 2B pencil and a cheap sketchbook. Forty bad ideas before lunch, two good ones by tea. You see all forty, because the bad ones explain the good ones.

02

Shout

We pick the loudest idea and test it tiny: on a phone, on a bus shelter, on a sticker the size of a thumbnail. If it whispers at 2 cm, it's out.

03

Cut

Vector, risograph, gouache or actual scissors: whatever makes the brand feel touched by a human hand. Final art is built to survive a cheap printer and a giant wall.

04

Stick

You get a living kit, not a PDF: logo, mascot in 30 poses, sticker sheets, patterns and a rulebook that is fun to break. Plus one call a quarter to check nobody's made it beige.
