The people who grew it

We visit every farm before we list it, and again every spring. These three supply about a third of what you see this week.

Duarte Orchard

Ines Duarte, 41 km away

"We grow 23 kinds of tomato. Verde is the first buyer who takes the cracked ones too, and their customers write to say those taste best."

Glasshouse 9

Tomas Ferrel, 12 km away

"Nine greenhouses, heated by the bakery next door. Our basil is cut the morning it leaves, and it still has its roots."

Underwood Cellars

Amara Okafor-Lind, 58 km away

"Mushrooms grown on spent coffee grounds from forty city cafés. Verde's vans bring the grounds back on the return trip."
