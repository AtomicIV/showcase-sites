How a forecast is made

From raw sky
to one sentence

01 · Observe

Listen to everything

Nine satellites, 212 radar sweeps an hour and 41,000 rooftop and lamppost sensors report in. Each reading is checked against its neighbours before it counts.

02 · Model

Rebuild the air, 1 km at a time

Our model splits the atmosphere into a 1 km grid, 64 layers deep, and moves heat, water and wind through it, learning from forty years of what actually happened.

03 · Ensemble

Fifty versions of tomorrow

We run it fifty times with slightly different starts. Where the runs agree, you get a confident forecast. Where they fan out, we tell you so instead of guessing.

04 · Explain

Then write it down plainly

A language model reads the ensemble like a forecaster would and writes the sentence you need: what changes, when, why, and how sure we are.
