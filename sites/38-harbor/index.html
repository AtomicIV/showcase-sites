A year in the life of one house

March · the spring storm

Wind took eleven tiles off the roof at 2am.

Water was in the loft by breakfast. The Ferreiras called at 7:12.

  • Emergency tarp the same morning
  • Roof repair and loft insulation
  • Two nights in a hotel while it dried

Paid in 5 days · $8,420

July · the kitchen fire

A pan, a phone call, and thirty unattended seconds.

Nobody hurt. The kitchen, less lucky.

  • New cabinets, worktop and extractor
  • Smoke cleaning for the whole ground floor
  • Food and meals while the kitchen was out

Paid in 9 days · $21,300

October · the break-in

They took the laptop, the bike and grandma's ring.

The ring was listed under Valuables. The laptop had the work stuff.

  • New laptop on Home office cover
  • Bike replaced like for like
  • Ring valued and paid in full

Paid in 6 days · $6,940

January · the burst pipe

Minus twelve outside. Rather wetter inside.

A frozen pipe split in the bathroom wall while the family was away.

  • Plumber within four hours
  • Drying, flooring and redecoration
  • Damaged rugs and books

Paid in 7 days · $12,780

    Questions

    Plainly answered.

    What does "mutual" actually mean?

    Harbor is owned by the people it insures. There are no outside shareholders, so profits either keep premiums down or come back to members as a dividend each spring.

    Is the price on this page a real quote?

    No. This is a concept site for a fictional insurer, and the calculator is a worked example to show how the cover choices fit together. Nothing you enter is stored or sent.

    How quickly are claims paid?

    Our median last year was six days from first call to money in the account. Simple claims, like a broken window, are often paid the same week.

    Can I insure a home I rent out?

    Yes — landlord cover adds loss of rent and tenant damage. It sits on the same policy, so one renewal date for everything.

    What isn't covered?

    Wear and tear, slow leaks you knew about, and anything deliberately damaged. It's all on page four of the policy, in sentences, without asterisks.
