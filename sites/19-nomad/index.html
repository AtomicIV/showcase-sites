One evening at Domo LengaTorres del Paine, Chile

4:10 pm

The road runs out

The last eleven kilometres are gravel. Rodrigo meets you at the gate with a thermos of mate and a map he drew himself, because the real ones are wrong about the river.

6:30 pm

A walk to the moraine

Forty minutes uphill to a ridge of grey stones the glacier left behind. From the top you can see the dome, the lake, and three condors who have clearly done this before.

8:45 pm

Lamb over the fire

Dinner is whatever Inés has decided, eaten at one long table with the other guests and whoever from the estancia is hungry. Bring a question; you'll leave with a story.

11:20 pm

Lights off, roof open

The dome's top panel slides back. No town for sixty kilometres in any direction, so the Milky Way is not a figure of speech. The heater stays on until you fall asleep.
