    Your first visit

    Four calm steps.
    No clipboard.

    01of four
    1. Step 01 · Before you arrive

      Arrive, don't fill in forms.

      Your medical history goes in online, at home, at your pace. When you walk in we already know your name — and whether you'd like the lights low.

      5 min online · 0 min in the waiting room
    2. Step 02 · The look

      See what we see.

      A gentle 3D scan replaces the gag-inducing moulds. Your whole mouth appears on the screen beside you, and we walk through it together.

      Low-dose digital imaging
    3. Step 03 · The plan

      A plan with prices, in writing.

      Options ranked by urgency, each with a fixed fee. Take it home, sleep on it, ask questions by text. Nothing starts until you say so.

      No pressure, no upsell
    4. Step 04 · The care

      Treatment, on your terms.

      Raise a hand and we stop — every time. Headphones, a blanket and a ceiling worth looking at. Then a text the next morning to see how you're doing.

      Follow-up within 24 hours

    Prices & questions

    The honest small print.

    Do you take my insurance?

    We bill most major dental plans directly and tell you your share before treatment. No plan? Our Lumen Membership is $29 a month and covers two cleans, exams, X-rays and 15% off everything else.

    I haven't been in years. Will I be judged?

    Never. Around a third of our new patients haven't seen a dentist in five years or more. We start with a conversation, not a lecture.

    What if I need to cancel?

    Reply to your reminder text up to 24 hours before and it's done — no fee. Inside 24 hours we ask for a $25 fee, which we waive for illness and emergencies.

    Do you offer sedation?

    Yes — from nitrous oxide ("happy air") to oral sedation for longer treatments. We'll talk through the options at your first visit.
