Good to know

Before you go

Kettle Valley is a working farm for the other 362 days of the year. Bring a reusable cup, leave the meadow as you found it and say hello to the sheep.

Getting there +

Festival trains run from the city every forty minutes on Friday and back on Monday morning. Shuttle buses meet each one at Kettle Halt; the walk from the bus stop to the gate is eight minutes, flat.

Camping and campervans +

Camping is included with every weekend ticket. Quiet fields close their music at midnight. Campervan pitches have a hook-up and are a little further up the hill, with the best view of the sunrise.

Accessibility +

Every stage has a raised viewing platform, step-free routes are marked in yellow and the accessible campsite sits next to the main gate. Carers come free; email us and we'll sort the ticket.

Kids and families +

Under-12s come free with a paying adult. The family field has its own toilets, a quiet tent with ear defenders to borrow, and the poster workshop runs every morning.
